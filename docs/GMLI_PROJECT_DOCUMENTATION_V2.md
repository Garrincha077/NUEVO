# GMLI 2.3 — Product & Analyst Documentation

## Objective
Global money/liquidity regime → asset transmission → strategic opportunity → entry quality → market confirmation → practical allocation/risk bias.

## Canonical architecture
### `/api/status`
Frozen source-of-truth blocks and freshness. Audit endpoint.

### `/api/decision`
No synthetic Core composite. Reports USD and FX-neutral separately, provisional current regime inference, Funding and an explainable 5-part conviction rubric.

### `/api/opportunity`
10-asset two-stage engine:
1. Strategic Eligibility
2. Entry Quality

Promoted Money channels:
- SPY → USD Money
- QQQ → USD Money
- GLD → FX-neutral Money
- DBC → USD primary, with frozen FX-neutral relationship retained in transmission definition

Research-only classes:
- TLT, HYG, VNQ, EEM, VEA, BTC

### `/api/positioning`
CFTC futures-only asset-specific positioning where mapping is direct.

### `/api/report`
Canonical ChatGPT/analyst contract. Combines regime, conviction, Opportunity Matrix, asset triggers, conflicts, freshness and research gaps.

## Evidence rules
CORE / OVERLAY / RESEARCH remain visually and semantically distinct. New research assets cannot silently graduate to Core.

## Time horizons
- GMLI regime: approximately 3–12M
- Contrarian Strategic Opportunity: approximately 12–36M
- Entry Quality: tactical staging overlay; does not override Strategic Eligibility

## Research philosophy
No indicator zoo. No silent retuning. Backtests can shape RESEARCH overlay design, but frozen Core changes require a formal promotion gate.
